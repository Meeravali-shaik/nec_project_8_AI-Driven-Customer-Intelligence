# AI-Driven Customer Intelligence and Predictive Analytics Platform

## Overview

This project is an AI-powered customer analytics platform that helps businesses understand customer behavior, predict future purchases, identify customer segments, detect churn risks, and generate business insights.

## Features

### Dashboard

* Customer KPIs
* Revenue Overview
* Customer Growth Trends

### Customer Explorer

* Filter by Age
* Filter by Income
* Filter by EMI
* Filter by Tenure
* Advanced Search

### Customer Segmentation

* K-Means Clustering
* Premium Customers
* Regular Customers
* Budget Customers
* Potential Customers

### Purchase Prediction

* Predict next likely product
* Probability score
* Cross-selling suggestions

### Personalized Recommendations

* Product recommendations
* Customer-specific insights

### Churn Prediction

* Churn risk score
* Customer retention suggestions

### Product Expiry Monitoring

* Subscription expiry tracking
* Renewal alerts

### Customer Behavior Analysis

* Spending analysis
* Purchase frequency analysis
* Product preference analysis

### Market Segmentation

* Age-based segmentation
* Income-based segmentation
* Location-based segmentation

### EMI Analysis

* EMI vs Income
* Financial health analysis

### Reports

* PDF Reports
* Excel Reports
* CSV Export
